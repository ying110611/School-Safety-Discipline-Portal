School Discipline Policy Highlights

Policy 01

Strict Anti-Bullying Stance

Physical, verbal, and cyber harassment are strictly prohibited. All reported cases will undergo direct review by the teacher-in-charge.

Policy 02

Whistleblower Protection

Students reporting infractions in good faith are fully protected. Identity details are strictly kept confidential between the student and discipline board.
